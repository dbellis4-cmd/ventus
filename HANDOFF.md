Virtual and stored recurring occurrences export as standalone events for a rolling year. A Google deletion disables only its linked occurrence; other series dates remain linked. Ventus wins simultaneous mapped-field changes; unsupported Google event edits pause their link. Disconnect clears the token while leaving Google events.

Retry/backoff state and safe reauthorization status are stored locally. The official `@googleapis/calendar` and `google-auth-library` packages were added.
